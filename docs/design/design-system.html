🎨 Colors

A pink-tinted canvas, never flat white

Every surface gradients from --color-bg into --color-surface-alt — a flat single tone reads as a generic template. Bubblegum pink is the only dominant accent; lilac and butter-yellow stay reserved for small reward moments so they never compete with it.

🔤 Typography

Rounded headlines, roomy body text

Headings run Baloo 2 (Quicksand as web-font fallback) — rounded and Vietnamese-diacritic-capable. Body copy stays on the system sans stack, sized above typical UI defaults so an early reader can scan comfortably.

🕹️ Buttons & Icon Controls

Pill buttons, floating icon circles

Every button is pill-shaped — no sharp corners anywhere in the system. Icon-only controls (back, fullscreen, zoom, done) sit at low opacity until touched, so the viewer image stays the star of the screen.

🗂️ Cards & Containers

Thick, soft, sticker-like surfaces

Cards read as "thick and soft" stickers rather than thin outlines — generous padding, a large radius, and a warm pink shadow instead of a neutral gray one. Bento-grid sizing on the homepage varies card-to-card rather than a uniform grid.

🐾 Motion & Mascots

Every topic gets a bouncing companion

Motion is the single biggest signature of this brand — a small animal mascot per topic, twinkling background sparkles, and gentle falling snow. All three run on transform/opacity only, and every one is disabled entirely under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce.
